# 🤖 AI Agents

## 📖 Definition

An **AI Agent** is an AI system that can understand a goal, make decisions, use external tools, and perform actions autonomously to complete a task.

### Simple Definition

An **AI Agent** doesn't just answer questions—it can use tools to perform tasks.

---

# ❓ Why Were AI Agents Created?

Large Language Models (LLMs) are excellent at generating text.

However, they cannot perform real-world actions by themselves.

For example:

```
Book a flight to Chennai for tomorrow.
```

Can a standalone LLM book the flight?

❌ No.

It can explain how to book a flight or generate a booking request, but it cannot complete the booking unless it has access to external tools or APIs.

Researchers wanted AI systems that could **think, plan, and act**.

This led to the development of **AI Agents**.

---

# 🌍 Real-World Example

Suppose you ask:

```
Send an email to my manager saying I'll be late tomorrow.
```

### LLM

- Drafts the email.

Done.

---

### AI Agent

- Drafts the email.
- Finds your manager's email address.
- Opens your email service.
- Sends the email.
- Confirms the email was sent.

The AI is no longer just generating text.

It is completing a real-world task.
