# Agentic AI

Agentic AI is one of the hottest topics in modern Artificial Intelligence. It extends the idea of AI Agents by allowing multiple intelligent agents to collaborate, plan, use tools, and complete complex tasks autonomously.

---

# Why Was Agentic AI Created?

Imagine a software company building an application.

One person doesn't do everything.

Instead, different team members handle different responsibilities:

- Business Analyst
- UI Designer
- Backend Developer
- Tester
- DevOps Engineer

Each person specializes in a specific task, and together they complete the project.

Now imagine replacing these humans with AI agents.

Instead of one AI doing everything, multiple specialized AI agents collaborate to accomplish a goal.

This is the core idea behind **Agentic AI**.

---

# What is Agentic AI?

## Interview Definition

**Agentic AI is an AI system in which one or more AI agents independently plan, reason, collaborate, use tools, and execute tasks to achieve a goal.**

### Simple Definition

Agentic AI is a system where multiple intelligent AI agents work together autonomously to solve complex tasks.

---

# AI Agent vs Agentic AI

| AI Agent | Agentic AI |
|----------|------------|
| Usually one intelligent agent | Multiple collaborating agents |
| Solves a single task | Solves complex workflows |
| Uses tools | Uses multiple agents and tools |
| Like one employee | Like an entire team |

---

# Real-world Example

Suppose you ask:

> **"Plan my 5-day trip to Japan."**

### A Single AI Agent might:

- Search flights
- Suggest hotels
- Generate an itinerary

### Agentic AI may divide the work among specialized agents:

### ✈️ Travel Agent
Finds the best flights.

### 🏨 Hotel Agent
Books accommodation.

### 💰 Budget Agent
Calculates total trip expenses.

### 📅 Calendar Agent
Creates a daily travel schedule.

### 🌤 Weather Agent
Checks the weather forecast.

Finally, all these agents combine their results to produce one complete travel plan.

# Why is Agentic AI Important?

A traditional LLM usually works like this:

```text
Question
   │
   ▼
Answer
```

Agentic AI performs a much richer workflow:

```text
Goal
  │
  ▼
Plan
  │
  ▼
Delegate
  │
  ▼
Execute
  │
  ▼
Verify
  │
  ▼
Complete
```

Instead of simply answering questions, Agentic AI plans, coordinates multiple agents, executes actions, verifies results, and completes complex workflows.

# Interview Questions & Answers

## 1. What is Agentic AI?

**Answer:**

Agentic AI is an AI system where one or more AI agents collaborate, plan, use tools, and perform tasks autonomously to achieve a common goal.

---

## 2. What is the difference between an AI Agent and Agentic AI?

**Answer:**

An AI Agent is a single intelligent system that performs tasks using reasoning and tools. Agentic AI consists of multiple specialized AI agents that collaborate, coordinate, and divide responsibilities to solve more complex workflows.

---

## 3. Why is Agentic AI more powerful than a single AI Agent?

**Answer:**

Agentic AI is more powerful because multiple specialized agents can work together, divide complex tasks, use different tools, verify results, and combine their outputs to achieve goals more efficiently than a single AI Agent.

---

## 4. Can Agentic AI use external tools?

**Answer:**

Yes. Agentic AI can use external tools, APIs, databases, and services through protocols such as MCP to perform real-world actions.

---
